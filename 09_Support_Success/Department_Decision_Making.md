# Support & Success — Department Decision-Making

## Internal Order
User_Support owns user-facing help; Host_Support owns host-facing help; the Support Manager (merged role at Stage 0–1) owns triage, SLAs, and the escalation line to Product/Community/T&S.

## Standing Contracts
| Support must loop in... | When |
|---|---|
| T&S | Any safety signal in a ticket (immediate, per routing SLA) |
| Product/Content | Recurring confusion themes (weekly digest) |
| Host_Success | Any host-capability complaint (same day) |

## Interface Rules
- Support never invents policy answers: uncertain questions get a ticket to the owning department and an honest "I'll get you the real answer" reply.
- Refund/credit decisions within policy are Small; outside policy escalate to Finance.
