# Product Goals — 01_Product

**Department mission:** Build the thinnest possible software that gets the most people to show up, return, and form real friendships — while keeping safety rails ahead of scale.

## Quarterly Goals (cascade from Company_Strategy Year-1 OKRs)
| Goal | Metric (Shared_Metrics.md) | Owning role |
|---|---|---|
| G1: Frictionless discovery→RSVP→check-in | Attendance Rate >70% | PM-CEX |
| G2: Squads & anchors that create habit | Repeat Rate >40%, Streak Continuation >50% | PM-SQD |
| G3: Safety rails precede scale | 100% hosts verified; report→action <24h | PM-SAF |
| G4: Evidence over opinion | Every shipped feature has pre-registered measurement | RES-PA |
| G5: Understand the first 20 minutes | Monthly research readout per activity type | RES-UR |

## Department Rules
- Feature requests without a named metric are returned (Ticket_Types.md).
- Product demos happen at bi-weekly Product Review; nothing launches without gate G2 quality sign-off from Ship_New_Feature.md.
- "Thin" is a feature: for every proposal, the default alternative is *subtract* something.
