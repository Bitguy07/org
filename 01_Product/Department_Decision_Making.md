# Product — Department Decision-Making

## Internal Order
Product Manager of the affected area frames → collects input → decides at Medium size; PM escalates Big items to Leadership Meeting with a one-page brief.

## Standing Input Contracts
| Before deciding on... | Product must hear from... |
|---|---|
| Any user-facing flow | Design (friction/anxiety), Community (ops load), Support (deflection impact) |
| Anything touching profiles, matching, check-in | T&S (abuse vectors), Privacy (location data) |
| Anything touching money | Legal (terms), Finance (billing ops), Growth (pricing experiment design) |
| Any experiment | Data (instrumentation + readout plan) before build |

## Interface Rules
- Engineering estimates are commitments, not suggestions; changing scope after handoff requires a CHANGE ticket accepted by Engineering.
- Product does not schedule launches during campus exam periods without Community sign-off (density protection).
- Any disagreement with T&S/Legal veto: per Escalation_Matrix SEV-2 — positions on paper, COO arbitrates, CEO final.
