# Design — Department Decision-Making

## Internal Order
Lead Product Designer arbitrates visual/UX coherence; area UX designers own their surfaces; all follow the department anxiety-assessment standard.

## Standing Contracts
| Design must involve... | When |
|---|---|
| T&S (DS-UXS + TS-SPP) | Anything touching reports, verification, profiles, location |
| Content_Design | Every shipped screen (words are interface) |
| Community | Any host-facing UI (hosts are non-technical users under stress) |
| Engineering | Feasibility before hi-fi; motion/perf budget at concept stage |

## Interface Rules
- Design handoff = frozen scope; changes go through PM-OPS as CHANGE tickets.
- No dark patterns, ever (P1–P3). Any growth request for one is documented and declined — logged.
- Every design decision memo answers: "How does this reduce time-to-arrival at a real event?"
