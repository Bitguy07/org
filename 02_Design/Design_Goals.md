# Design Goals — 02_Design

**Department mission:** Make showing up feel safe, easy, and quietly exciting — and make the app disappear once a user walks through the venue door.

## Quarterly Goals
| Goal | Metric | Owning role |
|---|---|---|
| Friction: discovery→RSVP ≤ 3 taps | Funnel conversion | DS-UXD |
| Anxiety: introvert-safe first experience | New-user attendance rate | DS-UXO |
| Trust: safety surfaces feel protective, not scary | Report initiation rate at events (target: healthy, not zero) | DS-UXS |
| Identity: brand reads "Gen Z-native, anti-corporate" | Unaided brand recall in campus surveys | DS-VB |
| Voice: every screen passes the "would a stressed student trust this?" test | Content QA pass rate | DS-CD |

## Department Rules
- Every flow ships with a written anxiety assessment (what could make a user no-show at this step?).
- Design review includes a "first 20 minutes" lens: we design for the venue arrival, not just the screen.
- Accessibility is a launch gate (contrast, screen readers, low-end Android).
