# Legal, Compliance & Risk Goals — 07_Legal_Risk

**Department mission:** Keep the founders out of court, the users out of harm's way, and the company's promises aligned with the law — at student-budget cost (templates, checklists, fractional counsel).

## Quarterly Goals
| Goal | Metric | Owning role |
|---|---|---|
| G1: Foundation documents | ToS, Community Guidelines, host agreements, waivers — written & current | LG-COUN |
| G2: Privacy compliance | Location-data handling documented; privacy-request SLA 100%; zero unapproved data categories | LG-PRIV |
| G3: Risk coverage | Higher-risk activity protocols with waivers; insurance quote by Stage 1; LLC filed at Stage 1 | LG-RISK |
| G4: Contract hygiene | 100% partners/vendors on reviewed templates; zero verbal deals | LG-COUN |
| G5: Legal review speed | Standard review SLA < 5 business days (blocking reviews flagged, never silent) | All |

## Department Rules
- We are enablers with veto, not the department of 'no': every review ends with "approved / approved-with-changes / here's a compliant alternative."
- Student budget is no excuse for legal debt on the four documents that matter most (ToS, guidelines, host agreement, waiver template).
- When in doubt, escalate outward to real counsel — these files guide agents, they are not legal advice.
