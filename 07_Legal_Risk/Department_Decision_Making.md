# Legal & Risk — Department Decision-Making

## Internal Order
Counsel owns legal positions; Privacy owns data-protection decisions; Risk_Insurance owns coverage and liability mitigation. Legal positions affecting policy or product are Medium decisions with the owning department (Legal advises + vetoes; departments decide within legal constraints).

## Standing Contracts
| Legal must be in the room for... | Always |
|---|---|
| New activity types (duty-of-care exposure) | Yes |
| Data collection changes (privacy) | Yes |
| Partnerships & vendor contracts | Yes |
| SEV-0/1 incidents | Immediately |
| Public statements about incidents | Yes — Legal owns external wording |

## Interface Rules
- Legal reviews are blocking: an unreviewed launch with legal exposure does not ship (gate G3).
- Legal advice is written (even one paragraph in the ticket) — verbal 'should be fine' does not exist.
