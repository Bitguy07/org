# Trust & Safety — Department Decision-Making

## Internal Order
Policy sets rules; Investigations enforces on cases; Safety_Product_Partner translates lessons into product requirements. Case decisions: TS-INV. Precedent-setting or policy-level: TS-POL with Legal. Product requirements: TS-SPP with PM-SAF.

## Standing Contracts
| T&S must involve... | When |
|---|---|
| Legal | Every SEV-0/1, every policy change, every new activity type, every external communication about an incident |
| Community | Any host suspension (they execute), any campus-level norms change |
| Product | Every launch gate G2; every safety requirement ships as a normal ticket with an owner |

## Interface Rules
- T&S decisions are documented in Incident_Log / Action_Log with rule citations — our work must survive legal scrutiny.
- Enforcement ladder is consistent: warning → restriction → suspension → ban, with appeal path always.
- T&S never communicates case details outside need-to-know; anonymized patterns are our public voice.
