# Trust & Safety Goals — 05_Trust_Safety

**Department mission:** Make "safe enough to go" the default feeling on every campus — and prove it with response speed, not promises.

## Quarterly Goals
| Goal | Metric | Owning role |
|---|---|---|
| G1: Fast response | P0 first action < 1h, resolution < 24h | TS-INV |
| G2: Policy ahead of scale | 100% activity types have written safety templates before launch | TS-POL |
| G3: Safety in the product | 100% user-facing features pass abuse review (gate G2) | TS-SPP |
| G4: Healthy reporting | Report rate < 2/100 events, reporter satisfaction > 80% | All |
| G5: Pattern intelligence | Monthly cross-campus repeat-offender review operating | TS-INV |

## Department Rules
- T&S holds standing veto (DECISION-2026-005). Veto use is logged and reviewed — vetoes are precious, never routine.
- Speed with care: containment first, judgment second, communication third — but all three always.
- We protect reporters absolutely: no retaliation, no exposure, ever.
