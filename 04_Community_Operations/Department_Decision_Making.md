# Community — Department Decision-Making

## Internal Order
Head of Community arbitrates across sub-teams; Campus CMs own their campus P&L-of-attention; Host_Success owns host lifecycle standards; Moderation executes T&S policy on content-level issues.

## Standing Contracts
| Community must involve... | When |
|---|---|
| T&S | Any host suspension, any event-format risk change, any campus where reports trend up |
| Product | Any host-tooling request (filed as FEATURE with host-voice evidence) |
| Growth | Any campus launch/expansion (density gate data) |
| Legal | Venue agreements, waivers for higher-risk activities, alcohol-related rules |

## Interface Rules
- Community is the **sensor network**: every host complaint, no-show pattern, and awkward first-20-minutes observation becomes a ticket or research request within 48h.
- Host suspensions follow the T&S path (COM-HS proposes, T&S decides) — Community never adjudicates safety alone.
- Community never invents events into a vacuum: partner with existing clubs first (Dependency Map).
