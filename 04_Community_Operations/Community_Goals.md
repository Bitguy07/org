# Community Goals — 04_Community_Operations

**Department mission:** Put real bodies in real rooms, week after week. Product gets people to the door; Community makes them come back — and brings friends.

## Quarterly Goals
| Goal | Metric | Owning role |
|---|---|---|
| G1: Density | 30 anchor events/campus, attendance >70% | COM-CM |
| G2: Host supply quality | Host activation >60%, host-attributed safety reports <1% of events | COM-HS |
| G3: The first 20 minutes | Post-event feedback ≥4.5/5 on "felt welcome" | COM-EO + hosts |
| G4: Healthy norms | Moderation response <12h; norms-violation repeat rate <5% | COM-MOD |
| G5: Community health visibility | Weekly community pulse report (qualitative + quantitative) | COM-HC |

## Department Rules
- Community data beats community vibes: every intervention cites a metric or a research finding.
- Hosts are partners, not contractors: we train, support, and — when needed — part ways respectfully.
- A campus with great events and weak safety is a campus about to die (P4). Community never trades safety for attendance.
