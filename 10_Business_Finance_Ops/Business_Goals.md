# Business, Finance & Ops Goals — 10_Business_Finance_Ops

**Department mission:** Keep the company alive, solvent, and operationally sound — from student-budget bookkeeping to the Squad Plus revenue engine.

## Quarterly Goals
| Goal | Metric | Owning role |
|---|---|---|
| G1: Runway visibility | Monthly cash report; burn vs plan (Stage 0–1: spend <$50/mo infra) | BF-FIN |
| G2: Clean books | Transactions categorized monthly; receipts retained; tax-ready | BF-FIN |
| G3: Revenue ops | Squad Plus billing live, churn tracked, refund SLA met (Stage 1) | BF-FIN + GR-PM |
| G4: Operating rhythm | Company ops calendar (rituals, filings, renewals) maintained | BF-BO |
| G5: People readiness | First-hire playbook ready at Stage 1→2 transition | BF-PO |

## Department Rules
- Frugality is strategy (P5): every recurring cost needs a metric it serves or it dies.
- Finance data is decision data: reports are written for decisions ("can we afford X?"), not compliance theater.
