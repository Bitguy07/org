# Business, Finance & Ops — Department Decision-Making

## Internal Order
Finance owns money decisions within budget; Business_Ops owns the operating system of the company (calendar, filings, vendor admin); People_Ops (dormant until Stage 2) owns hiring/retention. Spend beyond budget thresholds: Medium (dept head + Finance), Big (CEO + Finance).

## Standing Contracts
| Before deciding... | Involve |
|---|---|
| Paid tooling/upgrades | ENG-INF (technical need) + Finance |
| Pricing changes | GR-PM + Legal (terms) |
| First hire (Stage 2) | People_Ops playbook + CEO |
