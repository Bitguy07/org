# Growth & Marketing — Department Decision-Making

## Internal Order
Growth_PM owns the growth model (loops, gates, experiments); Growth_Marketing owns channels and campaigns; Brand_Content owns voice and creative; Partnerships owns external relationships. Campaigns under $0 (organic) are Small/Medium per agent; anything paid or brand-deal is Medium minimum.

## Standing Contracts
| Growth must involve... | When |
|---|---|
| Community | Every campaign (density gate + norms check); every campus activation |
| Data | Every experiment (pre-registered readout) |
| T&S/Legal | Anything user-facing at scale, any partnership with data sharing, any contest/UGC campaign |

## Interface Rules
- Growth metrics roll up to the North Star (WVA-R): a campaign that produces sign-ups but not attendees is a failure and is killed in Metrics Review.
- Partnerships never share user data without Privacy sign-off — partners get aggregate, anonymized insights or nothing.
