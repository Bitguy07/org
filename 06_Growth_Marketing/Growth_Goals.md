# Growth & Marketing Goals — 06_Growth_Marketing

**Department mission:** Grow density — never vanity. Every acquisition dollar (or hour) must land in a campus that can absorb it.

## Quarterly Goals
| Goal | Metric | Owning role |
|---|---|---|
| G1: Density-first acquisition | % new users from campuses at healthy density (100% — zero waste acquisition) | GR-PM |
| G2: Referral engine | Referral share of new attendees > 30% | GR-PM |
| G3: Organic content engine | Offline-moment content pipeline: 3 posts/week/campus, CAC ≈ 0 | GR-BC/GR-GM |
| G4: Partnership supply | 2+ club partners per campus, partner-attributed events > 25% | GR-PART |
| G5: Brand trust | "Made for students, not corporate" recall > 60% in campus surveys | GR-BC |

## Department Rules
- The density gate is sacred: no paid acquisition, no campus expansion marketing, before the current campus clears its gate (Dependency Map #4).
- We market real moments, not app features. The irony is the engine: students' offline photos are our best ad — earned, not staged.
- No dark patterns, no fake scarcity, no astroturfed hype (P1–P3, COM-HC veto applies).
