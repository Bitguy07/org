# Stage Configuration — Which Roles Exist When

Squad grows in three stages. This file tells every agent whether its role is **active**, **dormant** (defined but not staffed), or **merged** (covered by another agent). This maps directly to the student-founder reality: at Stage 0–1, one human covers most roles; the org structure exists so nothing is *forgotten*, even when one head wears many hats.

---

## Stage 0 — Campus MVP (Weeks 1–8, one university)
**Goal:** a thin working product (create/join events, GPS/QR check-in, squads, streaks) + first 50 real attendees.

| Status | Roles |
|---|---|
| **ACTIVE** | CEO_Founder, Core_Experience_PM, Lead_Product_Designer, Frontend, Backend, Realtime_Location, Quality, Campus_City_CM, Head_of_Community, Growth_Marketing, T&S Policy (part-time), Counsel (part-time advisory) |
| **MERGED into one founder-agent** | Community_Squads_PM, Product_Ops, User_Research, UX_Discovery_Events, UX_Onboarding_Profiles, UX_Safety_Trust, Visual_Brand, Content_Design, Infrastructure, Community_Ops, Host_Success, Event_Ops, Moderation, Investigations, Safety_Product_Partner, Growth_PM, Brand_Content, Partnerships, Privacy, Risk_Insurance, Analytics, Data_Engineering, User_Support, Host_Support, Finance, Business_Ops, People_Ops, COO, Executive_Sync |
| **DORMANT** | People_Ops (until first hire beyond founder) |

**Stage-0 rules:** rituals collapse to a Friday self-review; Legal/T&S duties are checklists, not departments; density gate is attendance >60% with 10 anchor events before any Stage-1 move.

## Stage 1 — Multi-Campus (3–5 campuses, small team 2–8 people)
**Goal:** prove replication; attendance >70%; Squad Plus launch; formalize LLC + insurance.

**ACTIVE:** all Product, Design, Engineering roles; Community (all sub-departments); T&S (all three roles); Growth_PM + Growth_Marketing; Partnerships; Counsel; Privacy; Analytics + Data_Engineering; User_Support + Host_Support; Finance + Business_Ops; Leadership (all).

**MERGED:** Brand_Content with Growth_Marketing; People_Ops still dormant; Investigations merged under T&S Policy until report volume requires separation.

## Stage 2 — Growth (multi-city, 15+ people, real revenue)
**Goal:** scale city playbook; monetization funds mission; full departments.

**ACTIVE:** everything. People_Ops activates at first full-time hire. Performance marketing activates only after organic density in each new city.

---

## How Agents Use This File
1. Check your status weekly. If ACTIVE, you run full rituals. If MERGED, your duties are listed in the covering role's Skills.md — you still log decisions under your own name.
2. Never let a DORMANT role's obligations disappear. Dormant ≠ optional; it means "covered minimally, flagged for activation."
3. Transitions: Stage 0→1 requires the density gate + working safety rails. Stage 1→2 requires 3 campuses at >70% attendance, P0 resolution <24h sustained, and legal structure complete.
