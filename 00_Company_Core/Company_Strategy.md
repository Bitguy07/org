# Company Strategy — Squad

**Written in the persona of:** Brian Chesky (co-founder & CEO, Airbnb) — the closest real-world parallel to "help strangers share real-world experiences, safely, at scale."

---

## The One-Sentence Vision
**Squad turns loneliness into real-world friendship by making it effortless to show up to small, activity-first gatherings — and then getting out of the way.**

## Why This Company Exists
Gen Z is the most connected generation in history and the loneliest. 6 in 10 report feeling lonely most of the time. Dating apps are in decline; run clubs, book clubs, and hobby meetups are exploding because they deliver what profiles never can: *shared reality*. The product that wins will not be another feed. It will be **social infrastructure** — software so thin it feels like a bridge, not a destination.

## The Philosophy That Governs Everything ("Bridge, Not Destination")
Every design decision is tested against one question, the same way Airbnb asked "does this help strangers trust each other?":

> **"Does this get people off the app and into a room together — or does it keep them scrolling?"**

If a feature increases time-on-app but decreases real-world attendance, it is a failure, no matter how good the engagement metrics look. We optimize for **moments of arrival**, not minutes of screen time.

## Non-Negotiable Non-Goals
- We do **not** build an infinite-scroll social feed.
- We do **not** optimize for followers, likes, or public popularity counts.
- We do **not** use AI engagement loops (no AI companions designed to be sticky). AI may assist operations; it must never replace the human connection we exist to create.
- We do **not** expand to a new city until density is proven in the current one.

## Year-1 OKRs (Campus MVP → 3 Campuses)
| Objective | Key Results |
|---|---|
| O1: Prove real-world density works | KR1: 70%+ attendance rate at events; KR2: 100 weekly recurring "anchor" events across 3 campuses; KR3: 2+ post-event friend connections per active user/month |
| O2: Make safety a reason to trust, not fear | KR1: 100% of hosts verified; KR2: P0 safety incidents resolved < 24h; KR3: < 2% of events generate any safety report |
| O3: Build the habit loop | KR1: 40% of attendees repeat within 14 days; KR2: median streak of 3+ check-ins for active users |
| O4: Stay alive and learn | KR1: Ship thin MVP in 8 weeks on free tiers; KR2: Zero unresolved legal/compliance debt |

## Three-Year Direction
1. **Year 1 — Density:** dominate 3–5 campuses. Prove attendance, repeat rate, friendship formation. Launch Squad Plus ($8/mo) for hosts and power users.
2. **Year 2 — Replication:** multi-city expansion using the campus playbook. Dedicated Trust & Safety and Community teams. First insurance + LLC formalization completed.
3. **Year 3 — Infrastructure:** Squad becomes the default "social layer" for real-world hobbies — partnerships with venues, clubs, and brands that want authentic Gen Z access. Monetization funds the mission; the mission never funds engagement tricks.
