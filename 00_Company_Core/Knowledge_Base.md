## Seeded Entries (from founding context — do not delete)
### [2026-09-01] Density beats features
**Author:** CEO_Founder | **Type:** Lesson
Local social products die from low density, not missing features. Rule: no expansion, no polish, while attendance <70% in the current campus.

### [2026-09-01] Activity psychology is not one-size-fits-all
**Author:** Product | **Type:** Lesson
Outdoor high-arousal events bond through shared mild adversity; indoor low-arousal events (book clubs, pottery) need stronger facilitation and safety cues or anxiety spikes. Design the first 20 minutes differently per activity type.

### [2026-09-01] The first 20 minutes decide retention
**Author:** Community | **Type:** User Insight
Users judge the whole product between arrival and minute 20. Host scripts, name tags, and arrival friction matter more than matching algorithms.

### [2026-09-01] This is not pure software
**Author:** Legal_Risk | **Type:** Lesson
Facilitating strangers meeting IRL creates duty-of-care exposure beyond Section 230 once we verify hosts, rate events, or claim safety features. Terms, waivers, and host agreements must be written before scale, not after an incident.

### [2026-09-01] Hybrid events are a trap
**Author:** Product | **Type:** Lesson
Virtual layers become the main experience and undermine the mission. Virtual is for coordination only.

### [2026-09-01] No AI engagement loops
**Author:** CEO_Founder | **Type:** New rule
AI may assist internal work (drafting, triage). It never owns a user relationship. A user leaving the app after finding an event is success, not churn.
