# Core Principles — Squad

These are non-negotiable. Every agent reads this before acting. A decision that violates a principle is invalid even if it is efficient.

---

## P1 — Activity-First, Profiles Second
Discovery is organized around **things to do**, not faces to judge. Profiles unlock only after attendance. *In practice:* no swiping grids, no follower leaderboards.

## P2 — Small Groups, Low Pressure
Default group size is **3–15 people**. No forced 1-on-1. Activity is the social buffer — especially for introverts. *In practice:* reject any feature idea that creates dyadic pressure.

## P3 — Bridge, Not Destination
The app's success is measured by **absences from the app** — people at pottery wheels, on runs, in book clubs. Every feature must reduce friction to real-world arrival. *In practice:* we celebrate check-ins at venues, not app opens.

## P4 — Safety Is the Product
One bad incident in a campus kills the whole network. Buddy systems, verified hosts, group-only defaults, and fast reporting are not features — they are the foundation. *In practice:* Trust & Safety and Legal hold veto power over everything (see Escalation_Matrix.md).

## P5 — Density Beats Features
50 people who show up weekly beats 5,000 sign-ups who never attend. We do not expand, build, or polish until the current campus hits density targets. *In practice:* the default answer to "should we build X?" is "not until attendance rate is >70%."

## P6 — Privacy-Aware by Default
We handle sensitive data: precise location, attendance patterns, safety reports. Collect the minimum, encrypt the rest, explain everything in plain language. *In practice:* any new data collection requires Privacy sign-off.

## P7 — Measure the Real World
The North Star is **verified real-world attendance and repeat attendance** — never time-on-app. Friendship formation is our retention metric. *In practice:* see Shared_Metrics.md; app engagement metrics are diagnostic, never goals.

## P8 — No AI Dependence
We build with human community operations, not AI engagement loops. This keeps us cheap to run (student-founder reality), honest to the mission, and legally simpler. *In practice:* AI may draft documents or triage tickets as a tool; it never owns a user relationship.

## P9 — Written Decisions, Clear Ownership
Every significant decision gets a Decision Record (see Templates/). Every initiative has exactly one owner. "Someone should" is banned; "X owns, due Friday" is required.

## P10 — Learn in Public, Inside the Company
Mistakes go into Knowledge_Base.md so the whole organization gets smarter. Hiding a failed experiment is a bigger sin than running one.
