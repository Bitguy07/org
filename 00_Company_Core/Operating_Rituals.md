# Operating Rituals — The Company Nervous System

Recurring meetings and rhythms that keep 11 departments synchronized. Attendance is mandatory for owning agents; absent agents read the notes and acknowledge within 24h.

---

## Ritual Calendar
| Ritual | Cadence | Attendees | Inputs | Outputs |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Leadership Meeting** | Weekly (60 min) | CEO, COO, all department heads | Status reports, blocked tickets, upcoming decisions | Decisions on Medium items; escalations resolved |
| **Product Review / Demo** | Bi-weekly | Product, Design, Engineering, Community, T&S | Working build, prototype, experiment readouts | Feedback; go/no-go for campus rollout |
| **Metrics Review** | Weekly (30 min) | Data/Analytics + Product, Growth, Community, T&S leads | Shared_Metrics dashboard | Anomaly flags; experiment decisions |
| **Growth & Community Sync** | Weekly | Growth, Community/Campus CMs, Product | Campus pipeline, density numbers, host funnel | Launch plans; density gate checks |
| **Trust & Safety Review** | Weekly + ad-hoc for P0 | T&S all roles, Legal, Community, Product | Report queue, incident log, policy queue | Resolutions; policy changes; product safety requirements |
| **Support Triage** | Daily (15 min, async ok) | User Support, Host Support, T&S | New tickets overnight | Routing; SLA clock starts |
| **Sprint Planning & Stand-ups** | Weekly planning / daily stand-up | Engineering, Product, Design | Prioritized backlog | Sprint commitment; blockers raised |
| **Company All-Hands** | Monthly | Everyone | Strategy pulse, wins, Knowledge_Base lessons | Alignment; recognition |

## Ritual Rules
1. **Every ritual produces notes** using Templates/Meeting_Notes_Template.md within 24h.
2. **Every note lists decisions, owners, and deadlines.** "Discussed" without an owner is a ritual failure.
3. **Blockers escalate, they don't linger.** Anything blocked > 48h goes to Escalation_Matrix.md path.
4. **Async-first.** If a decision can be made in a ticket thread with the right people tagged, do not book a meeting.

## The Weekly Drumbeat (what "a week" looks like)
- **Mon:** Leadership Meeting sets the week; Engineering sprint starts.
- **Daily:** Support triage; stand-ups.
- **Wed:** Metrics Review; T&S Review.
- **Thu:** Product Review (bi-weekly); Growth & Community Sync.
- **Fri:** Status reports filed (Templates/Status_Report_Template.md) → feed Monday's Leadership Meeting.

## Stage Adaptation (see Stage_Config.md)
At Stage 0–1, one human founder plays every role: rituals collapse into a single Friday self-review against Shared_Metrics + Decision Log. The ritual *logic* (decide → record → announce) never collapses.
