# Playbook — Launch a New Campus

**Goal:** a campus with 30 weekly anchor events and >60% attendance within 8 weeks of arrival, using the density-first sequence. **Do not skip gates.**

**Lead owner:** Campus_City_CM (COM-CM). Supporting: Partnerships, Growth_Marketing, Product, T&S, Legal.

---

## Gate 0 — Pre-Flight (T-4 weeks)
| Step | Owner | Ticket Type | Done When |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1. University policy scan (student org rules, liability, alcohol, data) | Legal Counsel (LG-COUN) | Change Request | Written go/no-go memo filed |
| 2. Safety rails checklist live (report/block, verified host flow, buddy system) | T&S Policy (TS-POL) | Task | T&S sign-off recorded |
| 3. Partner with 2–3 existing clubs (run club, book club, society) | Partnerships (GR-PART) | Task | ≥2 signed club partners |
| 4. Recruit 5 pilot hosts from partner clubs | Host_Success (COM-HS) | Task | 5 hosts verified |
| 5. Seed 10 anchor events (recurring, same time/place weekly) | Campus_City_CM | Feature/Event | 10 events on calendar |
| 6. Instrument campus dashboard (attendance, no-show, repeat) | Data/Analytics (DA-AN) | Task | Dashboard live |

## Gate 1 — Soft Launch (T-2 weeks)
- Invite-only to partner clubs. Target: 50 attendees across 10 events, attendance >60%.
- **Kill criteria:** if attendance <50% after 2 weeks, stop expansion, diagnose with User_Research, fix, retry once.
- Daily stand-up thread between CM, Host_Success, Support.

## Gate 2 — Open Launch (T0)
- Campus ambassador program on (5–10 ambassadors, referral codes).
- Growth_Marketing organic only: offline-moment content (photos from real events), club cross-promotion. **No paid acquisition before Gate 3.**

## Gate 3 — Density Proven (T+8 weeks)
**Expansion to next campus requires ALL:** attendance >70% sustained 4 weeks · 30 anchor events live · host activation >60% · zero unresolved P0/P1 safety tickets · repeat rate >40%.

## Rollback Rules
If safety incident (any SEV-0): pause new sign-ups, execute Handle_Safety_Incident.md, resume only after T&S + Legal sign-off.
If density stalls at Gate 1 twice: campus is put in "maintenance mode" — no new spend, events continue for existing users, revisit in one quarter.

## Post-Launch (T+8 weeks)
Post-mortem entry in Knowledge_Base.md · Decision Record for campus go/no-go on expansion · update Stage_Config if thresholds met.
