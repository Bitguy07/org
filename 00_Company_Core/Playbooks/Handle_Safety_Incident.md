# Playbook — Handle a Safety Incident (SEV-0)

**Applies to:** harassment, assault, injury, threats, doxxing, or any physical/psychological harm connected to a Squad event or user.

**Prime directive:** the affected person's safety and dignity outrank everything, including the company's reputation and growth targets.

---

## Phase 1 — First Hour (T&S Investigations owns)
| # | Action | Owner |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | File P0 ticket `INCIDENT-<date>-<seq>`; tag TS-INV, LG-COUN, LD-CEO | Any agent who learns of it |
| 2 | Immediate risk containment: suspend reported account, pause the event/host, preserve evidence (logs, messages, check-in records) | TS-INV |
| 3 | Contact the reporter with a human tone; confirm immediate safety; offer buddy-system follow-up and external resources | TS-INV + Host_Success |
| 4 | CEO informed (FYI, not approval) | TS-INV |

**Do NOT in hour one:** ask the reporter to prove anything, confront the reported user publicly, post anything publicly, or delay while "verifying."

## Phase 2 — First 24 Hours
| # | Action | Owner |
|---|---|---|
| 5 | Investigation: interview parties, review preserved evidence, check repeat-offender patterns | TS-INV |
| 6 | Decide enforcement: warning / suspension / permanent ban / report to authorities (with Legal) | TS-INV + LG-COUN |
| 7 | Communicate decision to reporter AND (at T&S discretion) affected community | TS-INV |
| 8 | Legal reviews exposure: duty-of-care, university policy, insurance notification if injury | LG-COUN |
| 9 | Product files safety-requirement tickets for any gap that enabled the incident | Safety_Product_Partner (TS-SPP) |

## Phase 3 — 72 Hours
- **Incident_Log entry** (facts, timeline, decisions — no speculation about motives).
- **Post-mortem** in Knowledge_Base.md: root cause class (host vetting / event design / product gap / bad actor), corrective actions with owners and dates.
- **Policy review:** does Policy/ need a new rule or activity-type restriction? If yes → Medium decision, T&S head decides with Legal.
- **Community care:** if the incident shook a campus, Host_Success runs a host check-in and CM posts clear, non-defensive norms update.

## Standing Rules
- Reporters are never punished for reporting, even if the report is unsubstantiated (bad-faith fabrication is handled separately and rarely).
- Repeat-offender signals across campuses are pattern-reviewed monthly (TS-INV).
- Every SEV-0 ends with at least one product or policy change. An incident with no follow-up change is an incident half-handled.
