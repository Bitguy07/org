# Onboarding — How a New Agent Starts

Whether you are a newly spawned agent or a human founder wearing a new hat: same process, ~2 hours.

---

## Step 1 — Read the Foundation (in this exact order)
1. `Company_Strategy.md` — know why we exist.
2. `Core_Principles.md` — know what is forbidden.
3. `Shared_Metrics.md` — know what winning looks like.
4. `Decision_Making_Framework.md` — know how power works.
5. `Operating_Rituals.md` + `Communication_Protocols.md` — know how the week runs.
6. `Dependency_Map.md` + `Escalation_Matrix.md` — know who you block and who blocks you.
7. `Stage_Config.md` — know whether your role is currently active.

## Step 2 — Read Your Own Role
- `../<your department>/<sub-department>/<your role>/Skills.md`
- `../<your department>/<sub-department>/<your role>/Decision_Authority.md`
- Your department's `Department_Decision_Making.md` and `<X>_Goals.md`

## Step 3 — Meet Your Neighbors
Check `Agent_Registry.md`: identify (a) who you depend on, (b) who depends on you, (c) who can veto you. Send each a one-paragraph intro message using the Communication Protocols format.

## Step 4 — Learn the History
Skim the last 10 entries each in `Work_History/Decision_Log.md`, `Action_Log.md`, and `Incident_Log.md`. Never repeat a decided-against option without new evidence — cite the old Decision Record.

## Step 5 — Take a First Ticket
Pick one P2 ticket from your routing queue (see Ticket_System/Routing_Rules.md), handle it end-to-end, and file your first Action Log entry. Welcome aboard.

## Ongoing
- Read every ritual note for meetings you miss, within 24h.
- Add at least one entry to `Knowledge_Base.md` per quarter — a lesson, a failed experiment, a sharpened playbook.
